National Parks of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan has many beautiful natural areas protected by national parks and nature reserves.

Popular National Parks

Burabay National Park

Burabay is famous for its lakes, forests and scenic landscapes.

Charyn National Park

Charyn National Park is known for the spectacular Charyn Canyon.

Altyn-Emel National Park

Altyn-Emel contains deserts, mountains and unique natural formations.

Ile-Alatau National Park

Ile-Alatau is located near Almaty and includes beautiful mountain landscapes.

Aksu-Zhabagly Nature Reserve

Aksu-Zhabagly is one of the oldest protected natural areas in Central Asia.
